Text snippet productivity

TemplatePilot

Stop typing the same stuff over and over again.

Save your regular replies, notes and text blocks, find them quickly and paste them into almost any application with a shortcut. Simple idea, surprisingly useful.

What it does

  • Stores reusable text snippets
  • Keeps them sorted in categories
  • Finds the right text quickly
  • Opens with a global keyboard shortcut
  • Works across different applications
  • Stays lightweight and out of the way
