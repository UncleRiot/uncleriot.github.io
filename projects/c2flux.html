Windows storage analysis

c² flux

See where all that disk space disappeared to.

No mystery, no endless clicking through folders. c² flux scans your drives, shows what is taking up space and gives you a few useful ways to search, compare and export the results.

View repository ↗Open the wiki ↗

What it does

  • Scans drives and folders without making a big drama out of it
  • Finds files and folders across the scan
  • Keeps scan history for later comparisons
  • Offers several views for people who like charts
  • Exports results to CSV
  • Adds handy Windows Explorer integration
